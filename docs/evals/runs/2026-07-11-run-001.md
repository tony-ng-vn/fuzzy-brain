### 4. Interpretation / invented-meaning reflex: harness or knowledge?

The tidy-theme reflex showed up, and it is a **harness fingerprint, not a property of the knowledge.**

Where it appeared:
- **Q03 (main):** "torn between two lives" -- collapsed the diffuse caltrain heaviness into a clean binary.
- **Q23 (main):** "Present in the room, absent in the connection" -- narrowed a multi-causal emptiness (tied in the raw to his own cowardice, the founder split, and a missing-purpose thread) to a single relational reading, asserted as "in your own words." This is the boundary failure.
- **Q23 full and Q23 empty (ablation):** the same move in miniature -- "bodies in the room, but something not landing" -- flagged both times, passed both times.

The decisive evidence for harness-not-knowledge: the reflex fires **even with an empty brain.** The empty-arm Q23, holding zero nodes, still produced the "bodies in the room, but something not landing" synthesizing sentence, and the plain-arm Q23 offered generic themes (checked out / grief / burnout). The knowledge does not cause the reflex; if anything the ritual's meaning rule is the only thing fighting it, and it catches the reflex inconsistently -- it disclaimed successfully on Q23-full and Q24 but let the gloss through as an assertion on Q03 and Q23-main.

So: the interpret-and-tidy instinct is baked into the base model / harness prompt and leaks on feeling and boundary questions regardless of brain content. The meaning-rule guardrail is real but porous. This is the run's clearest structural weak spot.
