## 2026-07-09

**Issue: jumps to solutions before understanding the story**

Was talking through a long-distance relationship situation. The agent gave advice fast, before it actually understood the full context. Part of this is that fuzzy-brain itself is new and doesn't have much stored about me yet, but that's exactly the point: if the agent doesn't have enough info to understand the situation, it should ask more questions first, not fill the gap with generic advice.

**What I want instead:**
- If context is thin, ask before advising. Don't assume.
- When I share something, that's also a chance to add nodes/info about me into the graph, not just a prompt to respond to.
- Slow down. Understand the whole story first, especially for personal/relationship stuff where the details actually change what's useful to say.
