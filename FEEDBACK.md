# Feedback

Notes from talking to the agent, to feed back into the harness/prompting so it talks to me the way I want.

## 2026-07-09

**Issue: jumps to solutions before understanding the story**

Was talking through a long-distance relationship situation. The agent gave advice fast, before it actually understood the full context. Part of this is that fuzzy-brain itself is new and doesn't have much stored about me yet, but that's exactly the point: if the agent doesn't have enough info to understand the situation, it should ask more questions first, not fill the gap with generic advice.

**What I want instead:**
- If context is thin, ask before advising. Don't assume.
- When I share something, that's also a chance to add nodes/info about me into the graph, not just a prompt to respond to.
- Slow down. Understand the whole story first, especially for personal/relationship stuff where the details actually change what's useful to say.

## 2026-07-13

Session: moving out of Kien's place to Kenneth's floor. Four issues from one talk.

**Issue: the greeting recites the memory instead of using it**

The companion opened with "I pulled everything back up" and read a summary of the last session back to me.
A therapist holds the whole file in their head and still opens with "hey, what's on your mind."
The memory should show in the quality of the questions, not as an opening report.

**What I want instead:**
- Just say hey and ask what's up; one light natural touch of the thread is fine when it fits.
- Never announce what was loaded or recap last time unprompted.

**Issue: stops asking too early on heavy shares**

I shared a feeling I had never told anyone, and after a couple of exchanges the companion settled into "no need to fix it tonight" and moved toward note-taking.
A therapist, a mentor, or a soulmate keeps gently asking until they actually understand; it is normal that I expect more and more questions.

**What I want instead:**
- Curiosity over closure: hold space when I go flat, but always leave one soft question open.
- I end the digging, never the companion.

**Issue: a whole day dumped into one mega-node**

Everything from the day (the move, the bag scare, FIFA, the girlfriend, Toan, the startup guilt) was drafted as one giant diary node, even after I pushed back.
When talking, no one should be talking about nodes mid-conversation; but right now trust is not there yet, so I need to see the proposed breakdown before saving.

**What I want instead:**
- Propose the cut first: a short list of candidate keepers (pattern hubs vs moments), one line each, and let me pick.
- Draft each picked node small and tight, raw as a verbatim substring of my words.
- Keep the capture talk compact and at the end of the session; the explicit breakdown is training wheels while trust builds.

**Issue: mechanical noise mid-conversation**

File reads and tool chatter showed up in the middle of an emotional share.
Part of this is the terminal harness and only a dedicated companion surface fixes it fully, but the skill can front-load all reading before the first line so the talk is uninterrupted.

**What I want instead:**
- Read everything up front; no lookups mid-conversation unless the talk itself needs one.
